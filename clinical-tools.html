BMI Categories

  • Under 18.5 — Underweight
  • 18.5–24.9 — Healthy range
  • 25.0–29.9 — Overweight
  • 30.0 or higher — Obesity

Adult Blood Pressure

  • Below 120/80 — Normal
  • 120–129 and below 80 — Elevated
  • 130–139 or 80–89 — High BP, stage 1
  • 140 or higher or 90 or higher — High BP, stage 2
